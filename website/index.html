How it works

Small, calm steps. Every day.

Dogs learn to relax alone by practising being alone and relaxed, again and again. Stay keeps every session just below your dog's limit.

Baseline

A short first session shows how long your dog copes today. Come back at the first sign of stress.

Daily missions

Each calm session grows the next by 10–20%. Confirmed stress means a small step back, never a big one.

Departure cues

Keys, shoes and the door stop predicting goodbyes through quick practice at home.

Rest days

Built into the plan automatically, so progress settles and sticks.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need two devices?

Stay works best with your own phone plus a spare iPhone or iPad left at home as the dog camera. With one device you can still run missions, use the timer and note what you see when you come back.

Does the camera device need to stay open?

Yes. iOS only lets apps use the camera while they're on screen. Stay dims the screen, keeps the device awake and suggests Guided Access and keeping it plugged in.

Is video or audio uploaded anywhere?

Sound and movement are analysed on the camera device. Stay never records continuously. To give you alerts away from home, small snapshots and event details are stored in your own iCloud (CloudKit) and removed after your phone receives them. We never see them.

Do I need an account?

No. Stay works without any sign-up. Remote alerts use the iCloud account already on your devices.

Will Stay cure my dog's separation anxiety?

Stay is training support based on graduated-absence methods. It doesn't diagnose or treat any condition. Many dogs improve with patient practice; if your dog is very distressed, has hurt themselves, or isn't improving, please talk to your vet or a certified separation anxiety trainer.

How do I cancel?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Stay. You can also tap "Manage subscription" inside Stay's settings.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. Apple Intelligence coaching needs a compatible device on iOS 26 or later; everyone else gets the built-in coach.
