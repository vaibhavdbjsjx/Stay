Setting up the dog camera

  1. On the spare iPhone or iPad, install Stay and choose This is the dog camera.
  2. On your own phone, open Settings → Dog camera to show the pairing QR code.
  3. Scan it with the camera device, place it where it sees your dog's resting spot, plug it in and tap Arm camera.
  4. Start missions from your phone; the camera begins automatically.

I'm not getting alerts away from home

  • Remote alerts need Stay Pro and iCloud on both devices (Settings → [your name] → iCloud).
  • Allow notifications for Stay on your phone.
  • Keep Stay open and on screen on the camera device; turn on Guided Access so it can't be closed.
  • Check the camera device has Wi-Fi and is charging.

The live view says "searching"

Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi with Local Network access allowed for Stay (Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network).

Too many or too few alerts

Change the dog's detection sensitivity in Settings → Dogs. Remember you can dismiss false alarms when you review a session; only confirmed signs change the plan.

Restoring purchases

Open Settings in Stay and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy.

Cancelling a subscription

iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Stay, or Stay → Settings → Manage subscription.

Deleting your data

Stay → Settings → Delete all data removes everything from the device. Unpair the camera to remove link data from iCloud.
