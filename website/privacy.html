Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

The short version: Stay has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your dog's data lives on your devices and, if you use remote alerts, in your own iCloud. We can't see it and we never sell it.

Who we are

Stay ("we", "us") makes the Stay – Dog Separation Coach app for iPhone and iPad. Questions about privacy: support.stay11@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

Stay stores the information you enter — your dog's name, photo, breed, age, questionnaire answers, training plan, sessions, reviews, journal notes and settings — in the app's private storage on your device. It is included in your normal device backups. We do not receive a copy.

Camera and microphone (Camera mode)

When a device is used as the dog camera, Stay uses the camera and microphone only while Camera mode is armed and on screen. Sound is analysed on the device by Apple's built-in sound classifier to recognise barking, howling, whining and growling. Video frames are analysed on the device by Apple's Vision framework to notice pacing. Stay does not record continuous audio or video, and audio is never stored or uploaded.

To show you what's happening, Stay creates small still images (snapshots) from the camera: a few frames per second sent directly to your phone over your local Wi-Fi, and an occasional snapshot sent through iCloud as described below.

iCloud (CloudKit) for remote alerts

If you pair a dog camera and use alerts away from home, Stay uses Apple's CloudKit to pass messages between your devices. These records are stored in a private area of your iCloud account (and, if your camera uses a different Apple Account, shared only with that account through an iCloud share you create by showing the pairing code):

  • mission start and stop commands, with your dog's name and the planned length;
  • a camera status heartbeat (armed or running, sound level, battery level, device name);
  • the latest camera snapshot for the near-live view, which is overwritten every few seconds;
  • detected events (type of sign, time, confidence, a short sound-level timeline and a snapshot), which your phone deletes from iCloud after downloading them.

Apple stores and protects this data under its own privacy policy. We, the developer, cannot access your private iCloud data. You can remove it by unpairing the camera in Stay, or in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Local network

On the same Wi-Fi, your phone and dog camera connect directly to each other (Apple's Multipeer Connectivity, encrypted) for a live view and instant alerts. This traffic does not leave your network.

Notifications

Stay schedules reminders and "time to head back" notifications on your device. Alerts from your dog camera are delivered through Apple's push service via your iCloud subscription.

AI coaching

Coaching tips and weekly insights are generated on your device, using Apple Intelligence's on-device model where available, or Stay's built-in rules otherwise. Your data is not sent to any third-party AI service.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details. Stay checks your purchase status on the device using StoreKit.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-up.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with anyone.
  • No collection of data by us: Apple's App Store privacy label for Stay is "Data Not Collected".

Your choices

You can change camera, microphone, photo, local network and notification permissions at any time in iOS Settings. In Stay → Settings you can export all your data as a JSON file and delete all data from the device. Deleting the app removes its local data.

Children

Stay is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
