Thank you for reviewing Stay: Dog Separation Coach.

**No login is required.** Every feature works without an account. Sign in with Apple (and optionally Google) is offered only to show the owner's name on trainer reports and when pairing a camera. Users can delete their account in Settings › Account › Delete account. "Delete all data" is in Settings › Your data.

**Quick demo with one device (about 2 minutes)**
1. Launch → Get started → "This is my phone".
2. Enter a dog name (for example "Biscuit"), answer the 7 short questions, then Continue → Not now → Continue without an account.
3. On Today, tap **Start baseline** → **Start baseline**. The timer runs. Tap "I noticed a sign of stress", then "I'm back".
4. Review the session (the manual sign is pre-confirmed) → **Save review**. The result screen shows the new plan. Tap Done.
5. Today now shows the first mission, length calculated from the baseline. Progress, Coach and Settings tabs show charts, coaching and options.

**Camera mode (two devices, optional)**
- Device A: "This is my phone" → Settings › Dog camera shows a pairing QR code.
- Device B: "This is the dog camera" → allow camera and microphone → scan the code → Arm camera.
- On device A start a mission: device B starts monitoring automatically. Barking or playing a dog-bark sound near device B creates an alert on device A, with a live view on the same Wi-Fi.
- The camera is only used while Camera mode is on screen; Stay never uses the camera in the background. All sound and video analysis happens on the device with Apple's SoundAnalysis and Vision frameworks.

**In-app purchases**
- Stay Pro: monthly ($6.99) and yearly ($39.99 with a 1-week free trial), both auto-renewable in one subscription group. The paywall opens from Settings › Try Stay Pro, or after the 3 free missions. It shows price, trial terms, auto-renewal text, Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links, and Restore purchases. Restore is also in Settings.

**Health wording**
Stay is training support based on graduated-absence methods. It does not diagnose or treat any condition, and it recommends a vet for severe cases.

**Privacy**
No analytics, ads or tracking. Data stays on the device. Remote alerts use the user's own iCloud (CloudKit private database / iCloud share).
